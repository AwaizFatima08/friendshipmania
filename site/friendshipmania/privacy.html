PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, in plain words

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

friendshipmania ("the app") is published by HomiLabs, an independent app studio in Pakistan ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Android app and this website. Questions about it: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

HomiLabs also has a studio-wide policy statement. If anything here is more specific, this page wins for friendshipmania.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. As a guest, we do not create an account for you and we do not store your names or results on our servers.
  • If you sign in, we store your account details and the verdicts you save, so they follow you across phones.
  • We never sell your data. The app shows small banner ads (by Google AdMob) on the verdict and "My verdicts" screens only, never during the quiz and never on the scorecard image you share. The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.
  • You can delete any verdict, or your whole account, from inside the app, any time.
  • The app is for people aged 13 and over.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Account details
Your name or nickname, email address, and a unique account ID. For password accounts, a password that only Firebase Authentication handles (we never see it). For Google sign-in, the name and email on your Google account (we never see your Google password).		 Only if you create an account or sign in. To sign you in, show your name in the app, and let you reset your password.
Saved verdicts
Your name, the friend's name as you typed it, the percentage score, the verdict title, the score for each category, and the date and time.		 Only if you are signed in. Each time you finish a quiz while signed in, one verdict is saved. To show your history in "My verdicts".
Technical data
Standard connection data such as IP address and device type, handled by Google as part of running Firebase.		 When the app talks to our Firebase services (sign-in and saving verdicts). To deliver the service and keep it secure.
Advertising data
Collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), not by us: your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance diagnostics for the ads themselves.		 On the verdict and "My verdicts" screens, when a banner ad is requested. In the EU/UK/EEA and Switzerland, only after you have made a choice in Google's consent form. To show ads, measure them, prevent ad fraud, and (only where you allow it) personalise them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

What we do not collect

  • Your individual quiz answers. Only the final score and category scores are saved, and only when you are signed in.
  • Your contacts, precise (GPS) location, camera, microphone or photos. We do not read your advertising ID ourselves; only Google's ads SDK uses it, as described above.
  • Anything about your friend beyond the name you type. We do not contact them and they do not need an account.
Please don't type sensitive information about other people as a "name". A first name or nickname is all the app needs.

Where it is stored

Account and verdict data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Verdicts are held in Google's Mumbai (asia-south1) data-centre region; Google may process account sign-in data on servers in other countries. Google acts as our service provider. See Firebase's privacy information.

Our database rules allow each signed-in user to read and write only their own data. Connections use HTTPS.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase) — to run sign-in and storage, as described above.
  • Google (AdMob) — the ads SDK sends the advertising data described above directly to Google to show and measure ads. Google uses it under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your account details and saved verdicts are never given to AdMob or used for ads.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • People you choose. The Share button gives your scorecard image and a short message to Android's share sheet, so you pick which app and which person gets it. After that, that app's own policy applies. The Download button saves the image to your own phone's gallery.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your account and saved verdicts until you delete them. We do not currently delete inactive accounts automatically.
  • Delete one verdict: in the app, open My account → My verdicts and tap the bin.
  • Delete your account and all data: in the app, open My account → Delete account & data. This permanently removes your profile, every saved verdict, and your sign-in record.
  • No longer have the app? Follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Copies in Google's internal backup systems can take up to about 30 days to disappear completely.

Your choices and rights

Ads: in the EU/UK/EEA and Switzerland the app asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in About & credits → Privacy & ad choices. Everywhere, you can turn off personalised ads or reset/delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads); ads will then not be personalised.

You can see your saved verdicts in the app, delete them, change nothing and stay a guest, or delete your account. If you are in the EU, UK or another region with data-protection rights (such as access, correction, deletion, portability, or objecting to processing), email us and we will help. We process account and verdict data because it is needed to provide the features you ask for (and, for optional sign-in, with your agreement, which you can withdraw by deleting your account).

Children

friendshipmania's humor is cheeky and is not made for children. The app is intended for people aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects (for example by adding analytics), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released. 18 October 2026: added banner ads by Google AdMob (version 1.0.1).
