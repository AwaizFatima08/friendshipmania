friendshipmania

Nobody lies to a polygraph. Answer 10 unhinged questions about your friendship, get a brutally honest verdict, and send it to the friend in question.

Get it on Google Play

Android only. The link goes live when the app is published.

How it works

  1. Type two names. Yours and your friend's. A nickname is fine.
  2. Answer 10 random questions. Raccoon kidnappings, goose attacks, zombie plans. It's different every time.
  3. Get your verdict. A percentage, a roast-y title and a category breakdown.
  4. Share it. Send the scorecard to your friend and dare them to take the test and tell you you're wrong.
