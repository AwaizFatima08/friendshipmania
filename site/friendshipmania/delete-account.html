ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your account & data

For the friendshipmania Android app by HomiLabs.

Option 1 — inside the app (instant)

  1. Open friendshipmania and tap your profile circle at the top right of the first screen.
  2. Tap Delete account & data.
  3. Confirm. If you signed up with a password you will be asked to type it; if you use Google you will be asked to confirm with Google.

That is it. Everything below is removed straight away.

Option 2 — you no longer have the app

Email homilabs.smc@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my friendshipmania account. We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner), then reply to let you know it is done.

Tip: if you still can install the app, Option 1 is faster and needs no email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name or nickname and email address).
  • Every saved verdict in "My verdicts" (including the friend names you typed).
  • Your sign-in record with Firebase Authentication, so the account can no longer be used.

What stays, and for how long

  • Copies in Google's internal backup systems can take up to about 30 days to disappear completely.
  • Anything you already shared or downloaded (a scorecard image sent to a friend or saved in your gallery) is on their phone or yours and is outside our control.
  • If you email us about deletion, we keep that email for a short time as a record that the request was handled.

Just want to delete one verdict?

In the app go to My account → My verdicts and tap the bin next to it. Your account stays.
