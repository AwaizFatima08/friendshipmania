Privacy Policy for friendshipmania

Last updated: September 12, 2026

No account No data collected No internet permission No ads or analytics

friendshipmania ("the app") is a lighthearted friendship-compatibility quiz published by Homilabs Solutions ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information — in short: it doesn't collect any.

The short version

friendshipmania does not have an internet or network permission at all — it physically cannot send data anywhere, because it never asks Android for permission to use the network. Nothing you type into the app is collected, stored on our servers, analyzed, or shared with us or any third party, for the simple reason that we have no server for it to go to.

What the app does with the names you enter

When you start a quiz, you type in your name and your friend's name. Those names live only in the app's memory for the length of that one quiz session, to personalize the questions and the scorecard on screen. They are never written to disk, never saved after you close the app, and never transmitted anywhere.

The scorecard image ("Download" and "Share")

FeatureWhat actually happens
Download Saves the scorecard as a PNG image directly into your device's own Photos/Gallery app, inside an album named "friendshipmania". This uses Android's standard photo-library permission, which you can grant, deny, or revoke at any time in your device's app settings. We never see or receive a copy of this image — it goes straight from the app to your own gallery.
Share Hands the scorecard image and a short invite message to Android's built-in share sheet, so you can choose which app already installed on your phone (WhatsApp, Messages, Instagram, etc.) to send it through. That app's own privacy policy governs what happens to the message once you send it — we have no visibility into or access to that step.

That's the complete list. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone state, or anything else — and it has no internet permission, so it cannot make network requests even if it wanted to.

Third-party services

None. friendshipmania does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, any advertising network, any crash-reporting service, or any login/identity provider. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

friendshipmania's humor is sharper and sarcastic ("roast-style") in tone and isn't intended for children. We do not knowingly direct the app at, or collect information from, children under 13 — and as explained above, we do not collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve a network connection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
